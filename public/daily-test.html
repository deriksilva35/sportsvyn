The rules

Somewhere in NFL history, one week has been chosen. You don't know the season. You don't know the week.

Draft five: QB · RB · WR · TE · FLEX. Every player offered actually played that week — but that doesn't mean they played well.

You get one attempt and 2:00 on the clock from the moment you see the pool. Your score is their real fantasy points from that actual week, worst pick dropped. After you lock, guess the season and week for a bonus.

⚠ Paper test build. Point values are approximate placeholders — verify against PROD stats before running with contestants.
