# NBA mock draft - sun-5, branch mock-nba (DEV only, HOLDING-FOR-GO)

## 1. Recon: how the NFL mock is built, and what NBA reuses
- NFL MOCK (/sim):
  - draft_configs presets with a draft per user (drafts / draft_picks, 046).
  - The pool is the FFC ADP snapshot (sim_player_pool, by scoring and team count).
  - Bots are lib/fantasy/engine.js aiPick (softmax over ADP plus roster need, canRoster).
  - The room is components/sim/DraftRoom.js (arm-then-confirm, timer, AUTO); results are
    DraftResults with a grade and the share card route /sim/draft/[id]/card.
  - Signed-in only: every pick is a server action writing draft_picks.
- WHY NBA DOES NOT RUN ON IT:
  - The engine, the slot vocabulary (QB/RB/WR/TE/FLEX/K/DST), the FFC pool and the configs are
    all NFL.
  - A signed-out reader cannot own a drafts row.
- REUSED:
  - lib/six/pool.js's readers through the stats feed's door (active rosters, season averages);
  - Game Drafts' look (app/draft/game/game.css: the navy header, tiles, pool rows, pills,
    result);
  - the share-card pattern (ImageResponse 1080x1920, the committed Saira font) and the arcade OG
    palette (lib/brand/ogPalette.js);
  - the cron pattern (advisory lock, recordRun, maybeAlert).
- NEW: a small pure engine (lib/mockNba/*) that runs entirely in the browser.

## 2. Built
- Migration 137 nba_mock_pool: one row per season, ADDITIVE. Applied on DEV only.
- Cron /api/cron/nba-mock-pool, daily at 10:41Z. It rebuilds the pool: about 13 feed calls,
  about 1.3 s. A pool under 180 is refused and the old one stays.
- POOL: active NBA rosters with a 2025-26 line, ranked by SV value.
  - SV = points-league FP/G x games played, capped at 82. The preset is PTS 1, 3PM 1, FGA -1,
    FGM 2, FTA -1, FTM 1, REB 1, AST 2, STL 4, BLK 4, TO -2.
  - 2026 first-round rookies are placed by draft slot: #1 just below the veteran ranked 40,
    then every 4th rank.
  - 260 players kept. DEV has the real pool: Jokic 1, SGA 2, Doncic 3; Dybantsa 41,
    Peterson 46, Boozer 51.
  - There are 29 centre-eligible players in the top 156.
- ROSTER: 12 teams x 13 rounds, snake; ESPN standard PG SG SF PF C G F UTIL UTIL UTIL + 3 BN (sun-6).
  - The feed lists only G / F / C (and pairs), so G = PG, SG or G; F = SF, PF or F; C = C;
    a pair is the union; anyone can play UTIL or the bench.
- BOTS: seeded, so a draft replays exactly.
  - Each bot picks among its legal players: the top 3 by SV, a bench pick worth 80% while its
    starters are open, weighted 60/30/10.
  - THE MUST-START RULE binds bots and the user alike: once a team's picks left equal its empty
    starting slots, only a player who fills one is legal, so no team ends with a hole.
- ROOM: /mock/nba is public and indexable.
  - Title "NBA Mock Draft 2026 - Sportsvyn", a description, a canonical URL, and a sitemap entry.
  - Seat picker (Random / Pick 1-12).
  - Navy header "Round N of 13 · your pick" and a 12-team scrolling strip with each team's last
    pick.
  - Tabs: Players (search, All/G/F/C chips, tap-then-confirm: lime row + "Confirm"),
    My team (13 slots, next pick), Board (round x team grid).
- SIGNED OUT: the whole draft is browser state and nothing is stored. So there is no guest row,
  no cookie and no sign-in wall - a simpler form of the Daily's "play first" pattern; there is
  nothing to claim afterwards.
- RESULT:
  - The grade comes from the team's place among 12 by team value (starters, plus bench at half):
    A+ A A- B+ B+ B B C+ C+ C C D.
  - "Steal: X, SV #n, taken Nth".
  - The roster with slots.
  - Share (text: grade, place, pick, link), "Get the card", "Draft again".
- SHARE CARD: /mock/nba/card?d=<grade|seat|ids>, 1080x1920, "MY NBA MOCK DRAFT", the grade, the
  13 players by slot. No session. A malformed payload 404s.
