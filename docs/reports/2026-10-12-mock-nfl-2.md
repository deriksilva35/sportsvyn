## Built
- Setup dropdowns: teams 8-14 (12), rounds (= roster), slot 1-N / Random, clock Off/30/60.
  Scoring PPR (default) / Half PPR / Standard / Custom (15 stat values, 2 decimals).
  Roster ESPN standard (QB 2RB 2WR TE FLEX D/ST K + 7 BN) / Yahoo standard (QB 2RB 3WR TE W/R/T K DEF
  + 6 BN) / Custom (0-4 per slot, bench 0-10). W/R/T and DEF are FLEX and D/ST by another name.
- Saved setups: mon-2's table with a sport column; one list per sport (same name allowed across
  sports, 10 per sport). League-setup presets: ESPN-style (PPR + ESPN roster), Yahoo-style (Half
  PPR + Yahoo roster).
- SVP = projected FP/G x games his team has LEFT. FP/G blends this season with last: under 4 games
  played 50/50, else 80/20; one season only = that season; no line in either = ranked after every
  projected player, in ADP order.
- Out / IR / Susp badges from the stats feed's injury report (575 rows: Out 197, IR 226 ...;
  Questionable gets no badge). Matched by name + team. Refreshed with the daily pool.
- Player card: 2026 and 2025 season totals (position-specific columns), FP/G each, the SVP math
  ("16.7 FP/G (80% this season over 5 games, 20% last season) x 12 games left = 200 points").
- Room: ADP / SVP sort, position filter incl. FLEX, auto-draft the rest (ADP + need, like the
  bots), restart, clock, grade = rank by projected points, share card "MY NFL MOCK DRAFT".
- /mock/nfl public, indexable, in the sitemap; the ADP source's attribution footer (licence).
- Daily cron /api/cron/nfl-mock-pool at 12:51Z (after the 11:00Z ADP snapshot).
