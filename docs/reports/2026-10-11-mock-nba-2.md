  need. MOCK_ADP_SOURCE=off hides ADP, sorts by SVP, bots use SVP.
- List eligibility (PG,SG ...) drives slots; G/F/C fallback when a player is not on the list.

## Found while building
- STALL: a custom roster with 2 C slots stalled at pick 204 - other teams parked the last centers
  on benches. New scarcity rule in legalFor keeps every open starting slot coverable (per type and
  per guard/forward group); checkFillable uses the same counts. 40-roster sweep test, all legal.
- With the 531-player pool no 0-4 roster runs short of a position (supply: C 112, PG 158 ...), so
  the only blocks a reader can hit are "no spots" and "bench only" (both with a clear message).
- The pool dropped anyone without a line last season: Haliburton, Kyrie, Lillard, VanVleet were
  missing. They are now ranked on 2024-25 (pool 450 -> 531, POOL_MAX 560).
- Auto-draft grades near A+ almost every time: the grade is SVP team value and auto-draft picks
  by SVP while bots pick by ADP. Not changed; see questions.
