## The rules as built
- MON-1 RULE (supersedes 65/35): age 25 or younger in 2026-27 -> 2025-26 only; 26 and older ->
  0.70 x 2025-26 + 0.30 x 2024-25 (no age known: treated as 26+). One usable season: that season.
  A season under 10 games is treated as missing (too thin to weigh) - my threshold, say if not.
- Age curve on the projected FP/G, by age in 2026-27 = the feed's age last season + 1
  (+2 for a player ranked on 2024-25): <=22 +8%, 23-25 +4%, 26-30 0, 31-33 -4%, 34+ -8%.
  The stats feed has no birth date, but its season averages carry each player's age; every
  veteran in the pool has one. Rookies: draft-slot estimate as before (no curve).
- Games: 75 for every player (your revised item 3); no penalty for last season's absences.
- "Out" badge: players listed Out / Out For Season on the injury report (18 today) keep their SVP
  and show a badge on the row (both sorts) and on the player card. The report is read when the pool
  is rebuilt (daily 10:41Z), so the badge is at most a day old. Day-To-Day gets no badge.
- The player card adds the projection line (weights, age, the age change) x 75 games.
- Traded players: no change (item 4); listed below.
