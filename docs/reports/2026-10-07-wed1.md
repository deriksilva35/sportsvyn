## 3. S2 league formats (branch league-formats-s2, DEV only)
- Migration 133 (DEV, ledgered) adds four player_leagues columns:
  - pick_format: regular | confidence | ats (ats reserved), default regular.
  - pick_format_prev and pick_format_from: the one-time switch's boundary.
  - pick_format_switch_open: true for every league that exists when 133 is applied, false for later ones.
  Values are lowercase, like the other player_leagues settings. DEV had 0 leagues.
- lib/leagues/pickFormat.js (pure) holds the formats, the lock, the switch and the record.
  - REGULAR scores wins from the lineup; a confidence board's points are re-counted to wins.
  - CONFIDENCE scores the board's own points. A board that was never ranked (before 20 Oct) scores its wins.
  - League totals are raw points. Under rank scoring, members are ranked on those points per unit.
  - Each contest is scored on the format in force at its lock, so earlier weeks stay as they were scored.
- Lock: any change is free until starts_at (the first counted kickoff). After that:
  - a pre-S2 league can use its ONE-TIME switch once. It takes effect from the 20 Oct 2026 week at the earliest,
    and never from a week that is already under way (one of that week's league boards has locked).
  - any other league is refused with "A change starts next season". Copy only; there is no queued
    next-season change.
- Create sheet: when Pick'em is picked, a "How should your league score?" step offers Regular and Confidence.
  The summary bar says "Confidence".
- League page: the W-L record sits under each member's total. A footer line names the format.
  The commissioner gets a switch with a two-tap confirm.
- Tests: pickFormat.test.mjs (12, pure) and pickFormatDb.test.mjs (6, DEV). The DEV file covers:
  - REGULAR and CONFIDENCE tables built from the same entries
  - the lock
  - the switch, which keeps the 13 Oct week as wins and is refused the second time
  - a league made after S2 never gets the switch
  - a legacy row scores as before
  The age-gate door count goes 45 -> 46 (setLeaguePickFormatAction is gated).
- Copy note: no league-format mock exists on the droplet. The card copy is mine, in PICK_FORMAT_COPY.
- Not in S2: the league-filtered Pick'em board (/pickem/<sport>?league=) still ranks on the public board's own
  scoring, not the league's format.
